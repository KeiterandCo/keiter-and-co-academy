Voice discovery is where copy quality is won or lost. If intake captures only facts, your copy will sound generic. If intake captures how the owner actually talks, what she cares about, and what she will not say, your copy sounds like a real business and not a template.

This module is in a working-session phase. What is here is the practical structure you can use now, plus a clear list of what the Olivia session will add.

## Voice questions to use in intake

Use these questions to hear real language, not polished marketing language:

- When someone asks what you do, what do you say first, in your own words?
- What are customers usually worried about before they call you?
- What do you wish people understood before they compare you to a cheaper option?
- What kind of work are you most proud of, and why?
- What words or phrases sound wrong for your business, even if they are common in your industry?
- What do your best customers say about you after working with you?
- If a neighbor asked who this business is for, how would you answer?
- What do you never want your website to sound like?

These are starter prompts for the interactive card deck coming in Phase 9.
