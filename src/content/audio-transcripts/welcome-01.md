What this is

The Keiter & Co. Academy is a curriculum that teaches you how to run a Keiter & Co. build from zero. It starts at "what is AI" and ends at "I handed a finished site to a real client and wrote the Welcome to the Family guide." Everything in between is documented here, in order, with the actual docs we use as source material.

This curriculum exists because Keiter & Co. has a methodology. A real one, built from doing this work with real clients in real communities. And a methodology that lives only in one person's head is one bad week away from being lost.

So we wrote it down. All of it. For anyone who comes after us, for anyone we bring into the business, and for ourselves, so we can look back at how we made a decision and remember why.

Who this is for

This curriculum is built for a future Keiter & Co. operator. That could be a hire, a contractor, someone Olivia brings in as the business grows, or Alex as he comes up to speed on the full methodology.

It also works for self-learners. If you found this published version and you build websites for small businesses, you will probably find something useful here. The methodology is practical and specific, and most of it applies to anyone doing this kind of work, not just people working under the Keiter & Co. name.

What this is not: a coding tutorial, an AI hype reel, or a shortcut to skipping the thinking part. The curriculum treats you like a smart adult who is new to this specific thing, not new to thinking.

What you will be able to do

By the time you finish all six parts, you will be able to:

- Explain what AI is, what Claude is, and choose the right Claude surface for a given task
- Hold the five Keiter & Co. standing principles without prompting
- Run an intake conversation with a real client and produce a brief
- Write a decision log, a spec, and a multi-phase kickoff for a small build
- Operate a build session inside the methodology: phase by phase, with handoff notes, against the full Definition of Done checklist
- Build to WCAG 2.2 AA, SEO, AEO, and online personality standards
- Hand off a finished site with a Welcome to the Family guide
- Support a Care Plan client month over month

If you stop after Parts 1 through 3, you will understand the philosophy and voice. If you stop after Part 4, you can build. If you complete through Part 6, you can run a Keiter & Co. engagement end to end.

How it is organized

Six parts, twenty-three modules.

Part 1: Welcome to Keiter & Co. Who we are, what we believe, the standing principles, and the partnership structure. Every decision downstream of it starts here.

Part 2: The tools. What AI is, what Claude is, and how to use it as a real build partner. The foundational layer.

Part 3: How we think. Voice and tone, the hard formatting rules, decision-making. The "thinking like Olivia" layer.

Part 4: How we build. The three-document system, phased prompts, context window management, build standards, Definition of Done. The technical methodology.

Part 5: How we run the business. Intake, pricing, proposals, infrastructure, launch, Care Plans. The operational layer.

Part 6: Practice. A real build walkthrough, a capstone, and the full reference library.

Each module is self-contained. You can read them in order or jump to a specific part if you already have some background. The prerequisites list in each module tells you what you need to have covered first.

How long it takes

Each module lists an estimated read time. The full curriculum, reading only, is roughly seven to nine hours. With the interactive components, it takes longer. The capstone build in Module 22 takes as long as a real build takes.

Most learners will not sit down and do it all in one stretch. Module-by-module over a few weeks is the expected pace. The mark-complete button on each module saves your progress locally, so you can pick up where you left off.
