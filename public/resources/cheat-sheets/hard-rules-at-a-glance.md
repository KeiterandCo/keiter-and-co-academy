# Hard Rules At A Glance

Keep this open while you write, review, or hand off work.

## Copy

- No em dashes.
- No contrast framing.
- No engagement bait.
- No therapy speak.
- No AI language.
- Use US English.
- Women first in references and sign-offs.
- Never invent information. Flag it or stop.

## Process

- We work with AI. It does not work for us.
- Agents can recommend, build, and automate. Humans push go.
- If something feels legally adjacent, stop and verify.
- End every phase with a handoff note.
- Add unresolved details to BUILDNOTES.

## Voice

- Warm.
- Direct.
- Unpretentious.
- Plain words first.
- Read it back. If Olivia would not say it, rewrite it.
